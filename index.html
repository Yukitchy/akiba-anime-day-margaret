01
Anime goods, many series

ANIMATE AKIHABARA

アニメイト秋葉原

Several floors of new anime and manga goods. This is the stop for Sakamoto Days and Frieren — new figures, books and limited goods — and there is plenty of One Piece and Pokémon too. The Annex building has a floor of capsule toys.

Philip likes Black Clover (Yami), so we will look for a Black Clover goods corner here and see what is on the shelves that day.

10:00 to 21:00

10:00 to 21:00, open daily (official site, checked on 10 Nov 2026)

Figures, cards, character goods

AKIHABARA RADIO KAIKAN

秋葉原ラジオ会館

A tall building full of small shops on every floor: One Piece figures on the figure floors, and Pokémon cards in the card shops. We pick the floors that match his favourites.

10:00 to 20:00

10:00 to 20:00 are the building’s hours; each shop inside keeps its own (official site, checked on 10 Nov 2026)

03
Second-hand figures and games

TRADER AKIHABARA

トレーダー 秋葉原本店

A big second-hand shop in its own building on Chuo-dori, a few minutes north of the station. Three floors of figures, sorted by series — One Piece and other shonen series on the 3rd floor, game and anime figures on the 5th — above two floors of games, from the newest releases back to Famicom-era retro. The photo shows figure display cases in an Akihabara shop.

11:00 to 20:00

11:00 to 20:00, open daily (official site, checked on 10 Nov 2026)

Second-hand manga, games, cards

BOOKOFF AKIHABARA EKIMAE

ブックオフ 秋葉原駅前店

Second-hand manga, game software and cards, one minute from the station. A good place to find the Frieren and Sakamoto Days comics themselves, and cheap games. The photo shows another Book Off branch.

Capsule toys

秋葉原ガチャポン会館

A shop with hundreds of capsule toy machines. There are always Pokémon machines in the rows — he can try a few and keep what comes out.

11:00 to 20:00 on Fridays, 11:00 to 19:00 on Sundays and holidays (official site, checked on 10 Nov 2026)

06
Trading cards, many games

BIG MAGIC AKIHABARA

BIG MAGIC 秋葉原店

A card-game specialist on the 9th floor of Radio Kaikan (place 02) — one of the biggest card shops in Akihabara. Pokémon cards alongside Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh! and many other games, with rows of single cards to hunt through. The photo shows a card game in full swing.

10:00 to 20:00

10:00 to 20:00 (official site, checked on 10 Nov 2026)

Google Maps →
07
Pokémon cards only

HARERUYA 2 AKIHABARA TOWER

晴れる屋2 秋葉原タワー店

A whole building of Pokémon cards, a few minutes south of the station — one of the biggest Pokémon card shops in Japan. Single cards from the newest sets to old classics, and a free play space with 152 seats. If he collects Pokémon cards, this is the stop. The photo shows a Pokémon card event.

10:00 to 22:00

10:00 to 22:00, open daily (official site, checked on 10 Nov 2026)

Yakiniku

Yakiniku Toraji

焼肉トラジ 秋葉原店

Yakiniku grilled meat and Korean dishes, one minute from the station. Lunch sets on weekdays, so Friday lunch is the easy way to do yakiniku.

Lunch 11:00 to 15:00 on Fridays
1 min walk from Akihabara Station

Gyu no Tatsujin

Yakiniku where every table is a private room, so it is the calm, slightly special option. On the 4th floor of the Akihabara SIL Building.

Yakitori and chicken

Kamadoka

かまどか 秋葉原店

An izakaya known for its fried chicken and kamameshi rice pots, on the 6th floor of BiTO AKIBA, the building right in front of the station. Yakitori and chicken dishes are the core of the menu.

Open 11:00 to 23:00, every day
BiTO AKIBA 6F, 1 min from Akihabara Station

¥¥ Lunch ¥1,000–¥1,999
