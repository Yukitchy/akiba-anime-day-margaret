Margaret, this page shows the plan for Friday 13 November: a birthday tour of Akihabara for your nephew. I pick him up at Shinagawa N Tower at 11:00, we take the train to Akihabara and leave his suitcase in a station locker, and we finish at Tokyo Station at 14:00, in time for his shinkansen to Okayama. The five places below are picked for his favourites — One Piece, Sakamoto Days, Pokémon and Frieren — and there are three model routes. Pick one, or we mix our own on the day. He does not need to buy anything.

Several floors of new anime and manga goods. This is the stop for Sakamoto Days and Frieren — new figures, books and limited goods — and there is plenty of One Piece and Pokémon too. The Annex building has a floor of capsule toys.

Lunch — beef or chicken

Margaret asked for beef or chicken, so lunch is a proper stop this time. Three places near the shops; we pick one together on the day.

  • Kanda Enzo — beef, in Kanda, a short walk from the shops.
  • Gyuta Honjin — yakiniku (grilled beef), on the 8th floor of Yodobashi Akiba, by the station.
  • Iseroku Honten — yakitori and chicken dishes, in Kanda.

If he would rather keep shopping, we skip lunch and stay longer at the shops.
